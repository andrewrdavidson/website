Why the wrapper is `relative group` and the hover is `group-hover:` (#32). The card needs a full-cover anchor so the whole box is one click target, and the anchor has to be a sibling of the card body — nested inside it, the anchor would swallow the chips and the READ POST button. But a hover that lands on the anchor then makes the *anchor* `:hover`, never the card body, so `hover:border-accent-line` on the body only fired over the action row (the one part that sits above the anchor). Grouping on the wrapper, which is an ancestor of both, makes any hover inside the card — anchor included — light the border. The action row stays `relative z-20` so the chips and the button remain clickable above the anchor. Styling and content are the homepage card's (#33): p-6, `rounded` (4px), no shadow, a text-2xl title, and the post's categories and tags in one row with READ POST on the right. Where the date sits (#14, revisited 2026-10-15). It used to be `float-left` under the title, with the description wrapping around it — a shape that read as "the date belongs to the description" at 56rem, and then read as a stray line of text on the left once the cards were 1.5x wider and the description no longer reached past the float. It is now the card's top-right cell: the same row as the title, hard against the card's right padding, directly above the READ POST button, with the calendar icon in front of it as before. The summary is a plain block under that row, so nothing has to wrap around anything and the `flow-root` that tamed the float is gone with it. The row is `flex-wrap`, and the title keeps a `min-w-[12rem]` floor on purpose: with a `min-w-0` title the row never wraps, and a 390px card ended up with the date beside a title column 127px wide — four lines for "Set DNS forwarders on all Domain Controllers". With the floor, a card too narrow to hold both puts the date on its own line, still right-aligned and still above the READ POST button, and the title gets the full width. It is the whole row that wraps, so every card on a page behaves the same way at a given width. `card-date` is the hook the position test measures and `post-card` the card itself. `post-card` is the card's own name, and it is load-bearing for the tests rather than for styling: `group` is a Tailwind utility the header's nav items also carry, so a `.group`-based selector silently means "the first nav item" to anything that reads it without a child combinator.
