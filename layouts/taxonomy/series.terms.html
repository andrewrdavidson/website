{{ define "main" }} {{- /* The /series/ index: every series, one card each, grouped under a year heading. The year a series is filed under is the year of its **first** post, and that is the whole point of this template's shape. It used to emit one year badge per series — the year of each series' most recent post — with an empty badge in practice, because the heading read `{{ .Key }}` off a dict that had no such key: the page rendered a full row of blank 4rem circles, one above every card. Asked for as "the series page [...] has the year marker on it but it's wrong, it should show once for each year showing the series' whose first post starts in that year under it, similar to this page /posts/". So: group by first-post year, one heading per year, descending — the same badge markup, the same `h1 → h2 → h3` outline and the same `ml-8` indent for the cards as layouts/_default/list.html, so the two lists read alike. Hugo cannot group a taxonomy by anything, and `GroupByDate` works on pages rather than on the dicts built here, so the distinct years are collected by hand and each group is filtered out of the same slice. Within a year the cards stay newest-first by their *last* post, which is the order this page has always used (the "series list needs to be newest first" fix, docs/BlogIssues-Fixes.md): the heading groups by when a series started, the order inside it still answers "what has been published lately". Keep `.category-count-badge` (assets/css/site.css retargets it for dark mode), the `year-indicator-badge` classes — the shared badge the /posts/ list and the term pages use too — and `.series-year-group`, which is this page's own hook for "one year heading and the cards under it" (the suite reads it to check the grouping). */ -}}
